                Core financial concepts
                The 50/30/20 rule

                A simple budgeting framework for your after-tax income.

                Put 50% toward needs (housing, utilities, groceries), 30% toward wants (eating out, hobbies, entertainment) and 20% toward savings and debt repayment. It gives you a clear path to financial health without tracking every penny.

                Pay yourself first

                The most powerful habit for building wealth.

                Before you pay bills or spend on anything else, set aside part of your income for savings. Automating the transfer to a separate account makes it effortless, and it counters present bias — our tendency to favour immediate rewards over long-term ones.

                Sinking funds

                Save for big, predictable expenses without the stress.

                A sinking fund is a small, regular saving for a specific future purchase, such as a car, a holiday or gifts. Breaking a large cost into small monthly amounts helps you avoid debt and the shock of a big one-off expense.
